Project OASIS

Voice-first computing · public beta, Windows 10/11

Three buttons, every app, your voice.

A three-button pad and a free Windows app. Hold a button and speak: OASIS types your words or runs the command, and each button changes meaning with the app in front of you.

  1. Hold one of three keys on the pad.
  2. Speak words to type, or a command to run.
  3. Done typed or executed in the app in front of you.
