# Surface: homepage (public/index.html)

Scope: personal homepage, Experience mode, built on local branch design/redesign (never pushed). Audience: investors and collaborators first; students and friends via conversation starters. Action: explore the person and the work, then start a conversation. Proof: real builds and statuses from PRODUCT.md only. Assets supplied by the user: public/now/FACE.png (portrait in visor glasses and white high-collar suit, transparent cutout; web copies face.webp / face-700.webp), DHANUSH_SCIENCE_LOGO.svg (delta mark resembling T, S and Greek delta), public/now/LOGO.svg (chrome render of the mark).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A white futuristic lab where the person is the product: a chrome delta mark, the portrait in visor glasses, and the equation of stationary action, delta S = 0, standing behind him as his sign. It refuses dark neon sci-fi and the hero-over-card-grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Clean white ground and near-black ink; chrome and thin-film iridescence (the logo's material) are the only colour, used on the 3D mark, the 3D OASIS pad and hairline accents. Sora for display, Geist for text, Geist Mono for data. Black fields for the quote and the conversation (the user's favourite colour). Square edges, hairlines, no gradients except the iridescent hairline.

STORY: The visitor meets the person and his sign, sees the flagship build as a real object, scans every build with status and origin, reads how he thinks, then starts a conversation addressed to who they are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top-left the live 3D chrome delta mark with the wordmark; nav top-right. Huge faint "δS = 0" across the hero background. The portrait cutout anchored bottom-right, large. Left: "Dhanush Science" in Sora, one positioning line, 'Start a conversation' and 'See the work', and a short note explaining delta S = 0. A thin build index along the bottom.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — user: "This is how I want the website to be... I want the website to completely look like a futuristic website... I have the logo PNG or SVG and the my photo wearing all the futuristic things"; background characters: "add the equation of stationary δS = 0 which represent the change". Signature interaction: the chrome delta turns toward the cursor and its iridescence shifts with the angle; the OASIS pad is a white ceramic object with chrome keys that does the same.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
